How to Play

Hold the highlighted area, slide to aim at an available teammate, and release to pass. Keep the ball moving, avoid defenders, and reach the touchdown area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pass Rush require an account?

No. Pass Rush does not require an account or sign-in.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. The current version of Pass Rush can be played offline.

How do I change music, sound, or haptic feedback?

Open the Settings menu inside the game and adjust the available audio and haptic options.

How can I reset my progress?

Game progress is stored locally on the device. Deleting and reinstalling the app removes locally stored progress.

I found a bug. What should I send?

Email us with a short description, the level where it happened, your device model, your iOS version, and screenshots or a screen recording when possible.
