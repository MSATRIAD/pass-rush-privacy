Privacy Policy for Pass Rush

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Pass Rush ("we," "our," or "the game") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Pass Rush mobile game.

Information We Collect

Pass Rush does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.

The game does not require an account or sign-in and does not use advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking technologies.

Local Game Data

Pass Rush stores limited game information locally on your device, such as level progress, unlocked stages, settings, and tutorial status. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

You can remove this local information by deleting the app or clearing the app's data through your device settings, where supported.

Device Permissions

Pass Rush does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other personal information. The game may use standard device features such as audio and haptic feedback solely to provide gameplay feedback.

Third-Party Services

Pass Rush does not integrate advertising networks, third-party analytics services, social login providers, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Pass Rush does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users. Because no account is required and no personal information is collected, users are not asked to provide personal data while playing the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
